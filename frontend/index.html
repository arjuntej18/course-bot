Automated course playback with recovery.

Start Automation

  1. Log in manually.
  2. Open the video you want to start from.
  3. Then click Start Automation.
  4. The bot will automatically open a new browser window.
  5. Log in there.
  6. Once you play the video, the bot will automatically play all the next videos.
  7. Keep the browser window open and do not close it until the bot finishes playing all the videos.
  8. If you close the browser window, the bot will stop and you will have to start over from the beginning.
  9. If you want to stop the bot, click the Stop button.
  10. Make sure you have a stable internet connection.
  11. If you want to restart the bot, click the Restart button.
  12. For safety, check the progress and checkpoint in the dashboard to make sure the bot is working properly.
  13. Don't disturb the newly opened tab while it is running.
  14. If you have any issues, please contact ravitejapersonal01@gmail.com .
  15. It wIt will take "frequent breaks between videos". Don't worry or stop the bot; this is normal.
  16. Enjoy the automation!
